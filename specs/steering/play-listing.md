## 2. Data safety form

Play counts data as "collected" when it leaves the device to the developer's server. The form is per app, not per mode: local mode collects nothing, but the account features do, so the answers below describe a signed-in user on the official server.

### Overview questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types? | Yes (collects; does not share) |
| Is all collected data encrypted in transit? | Yes. The official server is HTTPS only; release builds block plain `http://` (also for a custom server, see README) |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | Yes: in the app (Settings, Your data: "Delete my data", "Delete my account") and by email |
| Delete account URL | https://fletchr.de/en/delete-account/ |
| Can users request deletion of some data without deleting the account? | Yes, "Delete my data" keeps the account and teams |
| Shared with third parties? | No. Host Europe is the hosting processor, which Play does not count as sharing **[check]**. No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs |
| Advertising ID | Not used. The debug merged manifest has no `AD_ID` permission; check the release merge too **[check]** |

Further notes for the form:

- **Processed ephemerally?** No for all of the above; they are stored until deletion (backups up to about 12 months).
- **Google sign-in** (optional, Android, official server only): the server stores Google's ID, email and name; no Google token. Covered by Name, Email, User IDs above. The sign-in request itself goes from the phone to Google.
- **Android backup**: the app's local data can be part of the user's own Android backup to their Google account. That is a system feature the user controls, not collection by the developer **[check]**.
- **Custom server**: a user can sign in at someone else's server; that data never reaches the developer. The form describes the official server.
- **Local mode**: nothing is sent. Worth one sentence in the privacy policy (already there); the form itself has no field for it.

## 3. Content rating, target audience, app access, ads

### Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

- Category: **All other app types** (not a game, not social media as the main purpose) **[check the current category names]**.
- Violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling: none. Archery is shown as a sport with targets only; answer "no" to weapons-related violence questions unless the questionnaire asks explicitly about realistic weapons, then describe it as sports equipment without harm to people or animals **[check]**.
- Users interact or exchange content: **Yes**. Within an invite-only team, coaches and archers see each other's notes, photos and videos. No public posting, no chat, no stranger contact.
- Shares the user's location with others: No. Digital purchases: No. Unrestricted internet access: No.
- Expected result: a low age rating (for example PEGI 3 / USK 0), possibly with an "Users interact" notice **[check after submitting]**.

### Target audience and content

- Not a children's app. Do **not** select under-13 age groups: that puts the app under the Families policy, with its own rules for SDKs, ads and listing.
- Suggestion: **13+ (13 to 15, 16 to 17, 18 and over)**. Reasons: young archers in clubs are real users; the app already handles minors (video and profile picture of under-18s only after a coach has recorded parental consent; ranking off by default "for young archers"); nothing in it is unsuitable for teenagers.
- Alternative: **16+**. Fits better if you rely on consent under GDPR for account data: Germany kept the default age of digital consent of 16 (Art. 8 GDPR); whether consent is the legal basis here at all is a legal question **[check with the legal review, #63]**. It also matches the under-18 video consent less well, since 16 and 17 year olds would still need it.
- Known gap either way: a coach can keep a profile for an archer without an account, who may be under 13. The child does not use the app, but their data is in it. Mention it in the privacy review, not in the Play form **[check]**.
- "Could the store listing unintentionally appeal to children?" No (no cartoon style, no child-directed wording).

### App access

Choose "All or some functionality is restricted" and give instructions:

- The training log, statistics, equipment, photos, videos and notes work **without signing in**: the app opens on Home in local mode. Nothing to enter.
- Teams, coach notes, evaluations, profiles and the schedule need an account, and registering needs an invite code. Give the reviewer a test account on the official server (email and password in the Console's credentials fields, not in this repo), ideally a coach in a team with one archer, one profile without an account, a few sessions, a photo, a short video with a frame note, and one evaluation.
- The account must not use Google sign-in and must not need a second factor.

### Ads

"Does your app contain ads?" **No.** No ad SDK, no ad ID.

### Other declarations (answer No) **[check which ones the Console shows]**

Government app, financial features, news app, health apps declaration (if shown: no medical or health features; the fitness question as in the data safety form), COVID-19, data used for AI training.

- [ ] **Feature graphic**: 1024 x 500 PNG or JPEG, no alpha **[check]**. Name and a plotted target face; keep text minimal, it is cropped on some surfaces.

  1. Placing an arrow on the face, zoomed in under the finger
  2. A session with its ends, scores and group plot with the sight hint
  3. Statistics: average per session and arrows per week
  4. Heatmap on a 122 cm face
  5. Video player with a frame note and drawn lines and angles
  6. An archer's page from the coach's side (notes with visibility levels)
  7. An evaluation with criteria and a linked frame
  8. Equipment: a bow with its fields
- [ ] Tablet screenshots: not needed unless tablets are targeted.
- [ ] Use demo people and demo data only, no real archers (minors especially).

### Internal testing steps

1. Play Console: **Create app**. Name "Fletchr: Archery training", default language English (United States) or German, App, Free, accept the declarations.
2. **Set up your app** tasks: privacy policy URL https://fletchr.de/en/privacy/, app access (above), ads (No), content rating, target audience, data safety, government/financial/health declarations, category Sports, contact details, store listing (both languages).
3. **Play App Signing**: enrol (Google keeps the app signing key; `release.jks` becomes the upload key, see SELFHOSTING.md, "Release signing").
4. Put the SHA-1 **the installed Play build reports** (`adb logcat -d | grep GoogleProvider`, `signingSha1`, not the Play Console value; see `specs/design/accounts.md`) into the Google Cloud Android OAuth client, and the SHA-256 into `site/.well-known/assetlinks.json` for invite links (`specs/design/accounts.md`, SELFHOSTING.md). Without this, Google sign-in and verified `fletchr.de/join/` links fail in the Play build.
5. Build from **`main`** at the release tag, with the official server as default:
   ```bash
   git switch main && git pull
   VITE_SERVER_URL=https://app.fletchr.de VITE_GOOGLE_WEB_CLIENT_ID=<web client id> npm run build-only
   npx cap sync android
   cd android && ./gradlew bundleRelease
   ```
   The client ID is the same value as `DJANGO_GOOGLE_CLIENT_IDS` on the official server. Without it the build has no "Sign in with Google" button and nothing warns about it (the 0.15.0 Play build was made this way). Upload `android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`. The `versionCode` comes from `package.json` and must be higher than any earlier upload.
6. **Testing, Internal testing**: create an email list of testers, create a release with the AAB, release notes from `CHANGELOG.md`, roll out. Send testers the opt-in link.
7. Before production: new **personal** developer accounts must first run a **closed test** with a minimum number of testers for a minimum time before they can apply for production. Verify the current numbers in Play Console Help; internal testing does not count towards it **[check]**.
