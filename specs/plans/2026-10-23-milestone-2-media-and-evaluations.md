# Milestone 2: media and evaluations

Status: mostly implemented; open: trends and goals on Home (#46), averages per team, backups setup on the server (#49), video checks on a second phone (#71)
Date: 2026-10-23

- Evaluations (#44, #45): the server app `evaluations` has forms per team (`Template`, with versions: a change makes a new version, criteria keep their keys), evaluations (`Evaluation` with a date, kind coach or self, visibility, comment, goals) and scores (rating 1 to the scale, comment, optional clip and frame). A team starts with the shooting technique form (20 criteria in 5 groups, 1 to 5, in the structure of the DSB squad technique sheet, own wording; lazily made, in the language of whoever asks first). Evaluations are coach-only until the coach shares them (and can take it back); a self-assessment is read by the archer and their coaches; the export and "Delete my data" cover them. The app has a forms editor (Team, Evaluation forms), the evaluation form (rating buttons, comment, link a frame through the player), a list per archer (from the archer's page and from Home) and a detail page that links the frames. Open: trends and goals on Home (#46), averages per team.
- Photos and videos: the phone converts them (Media3, [video-prototype.md](../design/video-prototype.md)), the server only stores and serves them. Done: the prototypes (#35, #70) and the server side (#36: `media` app, `/api/media/`, permission-checked files with Range support, limits, quota). Photos (#37) are in the app: the Camera plugin picks or takes the photo, the phone shrinks it to 2048 px and strips the metadata, the upload queue (`useMedia`, files in the app's data folder) works offline and is generic for photos and videos. Added on `/media` from a session (everyone, and coaches for their archers) or from an archer's page (coach). Videos (#39): one Add sheet (take photo, record video, choose from gallery); a native plugin (Media3, `VideoConverterPlugin.java`) converts a chosen clip on the phone with the rules of `src/lib/video-plan.ts` (720p short side, real timeline, keyframe every 10 frames, size-capped bitrate, refuses clips over 60 s of real time), shows the first frame and the progress on the tile, and the small file goes into the same queue. Videos wait for Wi-Fi unless Settings says otherwise. Verified on the phone with synthetic clips (normal, rotated, speed 4, cut-off, garbage, cancel); not yet with a real clip through the picker, on a second phone, or for battery and heat. Player (#41, `/media/:id`): the clip is fetched into memory, the `<video>` plays it, and scrubbing, frame steps and the slider show single frames on a canvas decoded with WebCodecs (own MP4 reader `src/lib/mp4-samples.ts`, groups of frames decoded ahead in `src/lib/frame-decoder.ts`; without WebCodecs the `<video>` seeks, one seek at a time). The tape is one tick per frame, speeds 1x to 0.1x, previous and next clip of the session or archer. Gallery (#40): Home links to all your media, `/media` has a section per day, photos open full screen and swipe. Notes on frames (#42): a note can carry a clip, a frame and a time (`media`, `frame`, `time_ms` on `Note`, same visibility rules); the player has a notes sheet (chat button, count) where coaches add a note at the shown frame, a dot on the tape per note, the note of the shown frame under the readout, and a tap on a note jumps to its frame. Drawing (#43): a frame note can carry a drawing (`drawing` on `Note`: lines, angles with their degrees, circles, five colours; points are fractions of the picture, checked by `notes/drawing.py`). A coach draws in the player (pencil button: drag for a line or circle, three taps for an angle, undo), the drawing is saved with the next note, shows over its frame, and can be changed or removed from the note ("Edit drawing"). Single shapes cannot be moved yet, only undone from the last one. Open: compare, long clips and fast flicks (#72). Export and deletion (#47): the JSON export lists the media, a second button downloads a zip of the files (`POST /api/me/export/media/` gives a ten-minute signed link, the zip is streamed; the phone opens it with `@capacitor/browser`), and "Delete my data" and account deletion remove the rows and files, also what a coach added for the archer. Video consent (#48): a profile has `video_consent_at` and `video_consent_by`; for an archer under 18 (birth date known) without it, the server refuses new videos (photos are not affected) and the app hides video; a coach records or withdraws it on the archer's page (`PUT/DELETE /api/archers/<id>/video-consent/`). An archer without a birth date counts as allowed. How consent is collected (wording, join flow) waits for the legal review (#63). Not yet checked on the phone. The privacy pages (de, en), the delete-account pages and the in-app texts now describe photos, videos, frame notes and drawings, evaluations and the video consent; the legal review of the wording is still open.
- Backups of media (restic, off-site, #49): the script and the guide are done; the setup on the server is open (see Backups above).
