# Roles, teams and access control

Status: implemented (video consent wording and flow wait for the legal review, #63)
Date: 2026-10-23
Updated: 2026-10-30

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Archer** | Manage own equipment, log sessions and scores, upload photos/videos, see own stats, read coach notes and evaluations shared with them, fill in self-assessments |
| **Coach** | Everything an archer can see, for every archer in their team; write notes on archers, sessions, photos and video frames; fill in evaluations; team-wide stats |
| **Admin** (default: the person who creates the team) | Invite/remove archers and coaches, change roles, rename or delete the team |

Archers join a **team** by invite link or code. A coach can also keep a profile for an archer **without an account** (name, sessions the coach logs, notes); the archer takes it over later with a claim code, and the profile belongs to the team, not to the coach. One archer can be in several teams (e.g. club + squad); a team can have several coaches. Anyone can download everything the server holds about them as one JSON file (password required; it includes the coach notes about them, the coach-only ones too), and can delete their data (sessions, equipment and the coach notes about them) or their whole account, after typing their password and a confirmation word. The account cannot be deleted while its owner is the only admin of a team others belong to: someone else has to become admin first (in the app: Team, Manage team, Members). A team the person is alone in is deleted with them, notes they wrote stay as "former coach", and invites they made that nobody used are cancelled. A coach or admin who also trains can turn on "also shoots" for their membership in a team (off by default, only they can change it): the other coaches of that team then see their sessions and equipment, read-only, like an archer's. The setting is per team. Coach notes and evaluations are either **shared** with the archer or **coach-only** (default for notes: coach-only, so nothing reaches the archer by accident; evaluations: coach-only too, a coach shares one when they choose, so a poor rating does not reach an archer by accident). A coach sees all training data of the archers in their team that the archer shares with coaches (every session has a `visibility`, `me` or `coaches`, see [session-visibility.md](session-visibility.md); every coach-facing route reads through `visible_to`); coach-only notes and evaluations are visible only to the coaches of the team they were written in. **Every archer can write notes about their own training** (also one with no team): such a note has no team, is always private, and only its author reads it (not a coach, not a team admin). It can be about a session, an end or a photo or clip of the archer; the archer can change or delete it. It is in the archer's own data export (notes they wrote) and goes with their data when it is deleted.

A **profile picture** is seen by its owner, by the coaches and admins of the teams the person is in, and by the archers of those teams for their coaches and admins (not for other archers). Coaches of a team can set the picture of a profile without an account. For an archer under 18 it is held back, like video, until the parents' consent is on record. `core.access.can_see_avatar` holds the rule; everybody else gets 404.

Access control is enforced on the server in Django REST Framework (permission classes and per-user querysets on every endpoint, covered by tests): archers see their own records, coaches see records of archers in their teams. The same rules protect photo and video files.

**Opening a session for an archer.** A coach or admin can open a session for any archer they coach, with an empty type and without results, to attach photos, videos and notes (`PUT /api/sessions/<id>/?owner=`, the same reach as for media). They can later change its times and notes, never the type, ratings, equipment or results: those stay with the archer (or, for a profile without an account, with the coach as before). See [offline-sync.md](offline-sync.md).

**Video consent.** For an archer under 18 (birth date known) the server refuses new videos until a coach or admin has recorded the parents' consent on the archer's page (`video_consent_at`, `video_consent_by`); photos are not affected. An archer without a birth date counts as allowed.

**Team ranking.** The ranking of archers in a team is coach-only and off by default; an admin switches it on in the team settings (`show_ranking`), so young teams do not get a table that orders them.

**Schedule and training events (#104).** Every member of a team reads its slots and events. Coaches and admins write them (`core.access.can_plan`). A session is linked to an event by its owner (or by the coach who opened it, or the coach of a profile without an account); a note or a photo by a coach of the event's team for an archer of that team, or by the archer for their own photo (`can_tag`). A link adds no visibility: an archer still reads only the `shared` notes about them, the coaches the rest. See [schedule-and-events.md](schedule-and-events.md).
