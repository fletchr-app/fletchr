What data the app processes

  • Account: email address, name, password (stored only as a hash). Optional in the profile: dominant hand, dominant eye, date of birth and the units (metric or imperial). Coaches of your team see hand, eye and your age, not the date of birth; for a profile without an account that a coach keeps, the coach also sees the date they entered.
  • Training: sessions, ends, arrows, equipment.
  • Teams: memberships and roles, invites, the evaluation forms of the team.
  • Coach notes about archers, with their visibility. Notes marked "only me" cannot be read by anybody but the author and are not part of the archer's data export.
  • Profile picture (optional): one small JPEG that you or, for a profile without an account, a coach add. The app crops it to a square and shrinks it to about 256 px, and the server saves it again without any metadata (for example the location). You and the coaches and admins of your teams can see it, and the archers of those teams see the picture of their coaches and admins, not of other archers. For an archer under 18 it is only shown once the parents have agreed (the same record as for video). It is part of your data download and is deleted with "Delete my data" and with your account; a copy may remain in backups for up to about 12 months.
  • Photos and videos: files you (or a coach for you) add, with a small preview image, the time taken, size, length and a note. The app shrinks photos and removes their metadata (for example the location); videos are converted on the phone (without sound, at a smaller resolution) and then uploaded. You and the coaches of your teams can see them.
  • Notes on video frames and drawings: a coach can attach a note to a single frame of a video and draw on it (lines, angles, circles). They have the same visibility as the other coach notes.
  • Evaluations: a coach rates an archer with a form of the team (a rating per criterion, comments, goals, a date, and on request a video frame as evidence). At first only the coaches of the team can read an evaluation; the coach who wrote it decides whether you see it, and can take that back. You can also rate yourself; you and your coaches read that. Evaluations about you are part of your data export, also the ones that were not shared with you.
  • Consent for video: for archers under 18 (by the date of birth entered), no video is added until a coach has recorded that the parents consented. The time and the coach who recorded it are stored. The record can be withdrawn; videos that already exist stay until they are deleted.
  • Profiles for archers without an account: a name, notes, evaluations, photos, videos and sessions kept by a coach. The profile belongs to the team, not to the coach: it stays until a coach of the team deletes it or the team is deleted. When a coach deletes their account, the profiles stay with the team; if nobody is left in the team except profiles without an account, the team is deleted together with those profiles. The archer can ask a coach of the team to delete the profile, or create an account and take the profile over with a code (the sessions, photos, videos, evaluations and notes then become theirs, except notes that the coach keeps only for themselves).
  • Sign in with Google (optional, Android app, official server only): when you register or sign in with Google, the server stores Google's ID for your account (an identifier from Google, not a password or token) and your email address and name from Google. No Google token is kept. The sign-in request goes from your phone to Google; their privacy policy applies. If a Google sign-in is refused (for example because there is no account yet), the server log keeps one line with that Google ID and the email address; logs are deleted after 7 days. An account made with Google has no password.
  • Password reset: the email address receives a code by mail. Only a hash of the code is stored; it is valid for one hour and allows at most five wrong tries. Afterwards the server sends a mail saying the password was changed. The server sends these mails itself from fletchr.de, without a mail service provider.
  • Notifications (optional, Android app, official server only): if you allow them, the server stores your phone's Firebase Cloud Messaging identifier (a token), the app identifier, the app's language and the time it last checked in. A notification has a general text (for example "New note from your coach") and id numbers, no note content and no names. It reaches your phone through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (USA, EU-US Data Privacy Framework); Google's privacy policy applies. Without your permission nothing is sent to Google. You can turn each kind off in the app's settings or the phone's settings. Signing out, changing your password and deleting your account remove the token from the server.
  • Web server logs: IP address, time and requested address. They are deleted after 7 days.
  • On your device: a local copy of your sessions, so you can enter arrows without a connection, and your sign-in. Photos and videos that wait to be uploaded are kept in a folder of the app until they are uploaded or you delete them.
  • Without an account: the app also works on the phone alone. Sessions, equipment, notes, photos and videos then stay on the phone and nothing is sent to the server. When you sign in or register, the app asks whether to upload what you made without an account; only if you agree does it go to the server (you choose who can see it). When you sign out, the data of the account stays on the phone and comes back when you sign in again. Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone.
  • Android backup: the app's settings and its local data (sessions, equipment, notes) can be part of Android's automatic backup, which your phone sends to your Google account if you turned that on. Photos and videos on the phone are left out. Google's privacy policy applies to that backup; you can turn it off in the phone's settings.

What does not happen

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking services. Data goes to the Fletchr server. The exceptions are ones you start yourself: signing in with Google and the notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging (see above); the notification library stays switched off until you allow it.

Invite page

Whoever opens an invite link (fletchr.de/join/...) loads the page from this server. The page asks the app server what the code is for (the name of the team and, for a profile, its name). The server sees the visitor's IP address when that happens. The code is not used up by looking.

Hosting

The server is operated by Host Europe GmbH in Cologne. Its data centres are in Germany (Cologne) and France (Strasbourg), so within the EU.

Retention

Your data stays until you delete it or your account. After that a copy can remain in backups for up to about 12 months: there are daily backups of the last 30 days and monthly ones of the last 12 months. The backups are encrypted and stored on storage outside the server.

Your rights

Access and export, deletion of your data and your account: in the app under "Settings", "Your data". On request also by email to kontakt@fletchr.de. You can also complain to a data protection authority. See also Delete your account and data.
