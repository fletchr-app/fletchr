Delete your account and data

In the app: Settings, Your data. There you can download all your data, delete your training data, or delete your account completely. You need your password for it (or, if you sign in with Google, you confirm with Google).

What is deleted: your account with its sign-in and profile, your sessions with all arrows, your photos and videos (also those a coach added for you), your profile picture, your equipment, the coach notes and evaluations about you, and your memberships. Notes and evaluations you wrote as a coach about others stay and appear as written by a former coach; photos and videos you added for others stay with them. Profiles you kept for archers without an account stay with the team.

If you can no longer use the app, write to us from the email address of your account at kontakt@fletchr.de.

After that a copy can remain in backups for up to about 12 months.
