Fletchr

Training log, notes and statistics for archers and coaches

Fletchr is an app for archery training. You place your arrows on the target face, see your group and progress, and your coach can follow along.

The Android app

The Play Store listing is coming.

For archers

  • Enter ends and arrows right on the target face, also without a connection. The data is uploaded later.
  • Group, spread and progress per distance and face.
  • Arrows per week and average score over time.
  • Your equipment with its history: bows, arrow sets, and which setup you used in which session.

For coaches and clubs

  • Teams with roles: archer, coach, admin. Invite with a code or a link.
  • Coaches keep archers who have no account, write notes and log sessions. The archer takes the profile over later with a code.
  • Notes only for yourself, for all coaches of the team, or shared with the archer.
  • A team overview: who trained last, and how many arrows.

Your data

No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. Export and deletion of your data and your account are in the app. More in the privacy policy.
